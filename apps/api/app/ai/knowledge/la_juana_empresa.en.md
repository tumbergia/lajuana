## 1. General description

We are a nature and culture tourism agency specialized in mule rides within the Coffee Cultural Landscape, in the municipality of Neira (Caldas), Colombia.

Our focus is immersive experiences that combine:

- Mules
- Nature
- Culture

## 2. Nature of the service

We operate as an experiential tourism provider focused on equestrian activities, with the mule as the main way to explore rural and mountainous territory.

We design experiences for different levels:

- Basic (beginner)
- Intermediate
- Advanced

## 3. Value proposition

We want you to live experiences that:

- Connect you with Colombian coffee culture
- Take you into less crowded rural areas
- Bring you closer to nature and local communities

The mule is central for us because of its:

- Adaptation to mountain terrain
- Endurance and safety on rural trails
- Historical role in Colombian muleteering (arriería)

## 4. Service portfolio

### Main experiences

We offer:

- Half-day rides
- Full-day rides
- Custom multi-day routes

Our experiences usually include:

- Specialized guiding
- Full logistics
- Equipped mule
- Traditional food
- Accident insurance

### Complementary services

We can also help arrange:

- Heritage town visits
- Coffee experiences
- Gastronomic tourism
- Hot springs
- Natural parks
- Lodging and transport with partners

## 5. Location and territorial context

We are based in:

- Municipality: Neira (Caldas)
- Region: Coffee Axis (Eje Cafetero)
- Nearby: about 30 minutes from Manizales

We operate inside the Coffee Cultural Landscape (UNESCO). That setting is the heart of what we share with you.

## 5b. Opening hours

We are open every day from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. (Colombia time):

- Monday to Sunday: 8:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.

## 6. Our origins

We were born from the initiative of our founders, Jairo and Pamela, who bring together:

- Technical knowledge of equines

We grew from a desire to share the culture of mules and rural territory.

## 7. Main operating asset

At our core is a “mulada” (our group of mules) that we:

- Select and train
- Care for under controlled welfare conditions
- Orient toward safety and stability for you on the trail

## 8. Sustainability approach

We work with practices focused on:

- Equine welfare
- Direct relationships with local communities
- Conserving natural and cultural heritage

## 9. Positioning

We see ourselves as a specialized tourism operator in:

- Authentic rural experiences
- Nature tourism
- Cultural tourism

With a clear difference: the mule as the axis of the experience.
